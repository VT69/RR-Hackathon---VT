## 4. Cricket-correctness checklist

Column semantics, established from the data and confirmed by the reconciliation in §4.8:

- `runs` = runs off the bat (credited to the striker).
- `extras1_runs` = the primary extra: all wide runs, the 1-run no-ball penalty, byes, leg-byes, or penalty runs.
- `extras_2_runs` = byes/leg-byes run on top of a no-ball.
- `extras_type`: `wide` 7,714 · `legbyes` 2,748 · `Overstep` 576 · `byes` 529 · `other` 264 · `Penalty` 1.

| # | Check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | **Balls faced** | Wides never carry bat runs (0 cases) → not a ball faced. `Overstep` **and** `other` are both no-balls: every one carries exactly 1 penalty run and is re-bowled. Treating both as illegal gives exactly 6 legal balls in **30,622 of 30,626** completed overs; treating `other` as legal breaks 217 overs. No-balls count as faced, not as legal. |
| 4.2 | **Runs charged to bowler** | `runs` + `extras1_runs` on wides and no-balls. Byes, leg-byes, `extras_2_runs` and penalty runs are excluded. |
| 4.3 | **Bowler wickets** | Credited: Caught 7,557, Bowled 1,775, LBW 709, Stump 214, Hit Wicket 17. Not credited: RunOut 600, Retire Out 6, Obstructing the field 3 (spelled two ways). 40 wickets fall on wides (25 stumpings, 15 run-outs) and 8 on no-balls (all run-outs). |
| 4.4 | **Dismissed batter v striker** | `dismissed_player_id` identifies who is out. In **271 of 600 run-outs (45%) the non-striker is out**, plus 2 retire-outs and 1 obstruction. One retire-out row (match 40709) has no dismissed id. |
| 4.5 | **Retired hurt** | Not a dismissal type. Encoded as `dismissed_player_id` set with `is_wicket = false` — 12 rows. Not a wicket for the team or bowler; the batter simply leaves. |
| 4.6 | **Super overs** | Stored as innings 3 and 4: 38 balls in 6 matches. Nine matches finished level after 20 overs; three of them (13613, 21176, 28962) have **no super-over balls** but do have duplicated playing-XI rows (the 40 duplicates). |
| 4.7 | **Legal-ball integrity** | No innings exceeds 120 legal balls. Four completed overs are off by one ball (match 14130 over 14 in both innings, 14164 over 14, 41509 over 12 has 7). 35 overs list two bowlers (mid-over replacements). One bowler has 25 legal balls in an innings (the 7-ball over). |
| 4.8 | **Totals reproduce results** | Checkable matches are those with two innings, no rain revision, a numeric margin in the result text and unequal totals (tied matches quote the super-over margin, so they are excluded). Of 399 won by runs, the ball-level margin is exact in 398; of 406 won by wickets, exact in 405. The toss decision matches the team batting first in 100% of matches. |

**The two matches that do not reconcile — both are source-data errors, neither is a rule error:**

- **14130** (LSG v MI, 2022-04-24; result "won by 36 runs", ball data gives 167 − 130 = 37). Over 14 has only five legal deliveries in *both* innings, so one delivery is missing from each innings in the source file. Every other over in the match has six. The one-run gap is the difference between the two missing balls.
- **20190** (LSG v SRH, 2023-04-07; result "won by 6 wickets", ball data gives 5 wickets down). The ball data is internally consistent with five wickets: five dismissal rows, each a different batter, and exactly seven batters come to the crease in the chase (five out + two not out; six wickets would need eight). The result string in `matches.csv` is the error.

An earlier version of this check reported 804 of 807. It wrongly included two tied matches (28962, 34429) whose result text gives the super-over margin; one of them "matched" by coincidence. Removing them gives 803 of 805.

These rules are implemented once in `src/data/cleaning.py::add_ball_flags` and covered by 9 unit tests in `tests/test_ball_flags.py`.

Residual row-level oddities (too few to move anything, left as is): 3 innings each missing one delivery (14130 both innings, 14164 first innings); 2 untyped rows carrying 1 extra run; 1 leg-bye row with the run in `runs`; 23 fours/sixes not flagged as boundaries (all-run or overthrows); 4 second-innings balls recorded after the target was passed; 1 row where striker = non-striker; 1 `Penalty` row (5 runs, occupies a ball slot).

## 6. Impact Player (IPL 2023+)

`is_impact` / `is_impact_off` mark the substitution: 139, 136, 142 and 141 subs in IPL 2023–2026 (about 1.9 per match, i.e. almost every team, every match) and none in 2022 or in any international. 552 IPL team-matches list 12 players as having played. Batting depth in IPL 2023+ is therefore effectively one deeper than in IPL 2022 or T20Is, so a wicket in hand is worth slightly less there. Six batter/bowler appearances are not in the played XI (substitute edge cases).

## 9. Other notes

- `delivery_ist_time` is labelled UTC despite the name; 4 matches have date-only stamps and 4 balls go backwards in time. Ordering uses `innings_ball_num`, never the timestamp.
- `batting_order` in the ball data is constant per batter per innings; values 12–13 appear in 3 matches (substitutes / super-over artefacts).
- `season` uses split years (`2022/23`) for 18 matches; calendar year from `match_date` is used instead.
